# Tanzimat Reforms Monopoly

Welcome to **Tanzimat Reforms Monopoly** — a unique blend of classic Monopoly gameplay and Ottoman Empire history!  
Travel around the board, purchase reform properties, and test your knowledge of the Tanzimat era (1839–1876) to earn Knowledge Points (KP). The player who modernizes the empire most effectively wins!

---

## 🎮 Game Overview

In this educational twist on Monopoly, you become a 19th-century Ottoman reformer. Your goal is to acquire key reform spaces, answer historical questions correctly, and accumulate wealth (Knowledge Points). The game combines:

- **Property trading** (with themed reform categories)
- **Chance & Community Chest** cards inspired by Ottoman history
- **Trivia questions** that award bonus points
- **Classic Monopoly movement** (dice rolls, jail, passing GO, etc.)

The board is fully interactive, and each space features a real Tanzimat-related concept (e.g., *Rose Garden Edict*, *Secular Courts*, *Constitution*). Answering questions on purchased properties deepens your understanding while boosting your score.

---

## 🚀 How to Play

### 1. Setup
- Choose **2–4 players**.
- Give each player a **name** and **color**.
- Click **“Start Reform Game”**.

### 2. Game Flow
- Players take turns rolling the dice.
- Move around the 40‑space board.
- Land on:
  - **Property** – buy it (if unowned) and answer a category question to earn extra KP.
  - **Tax / Railroad / Utility** – pay rent or fees.
  - **Chance / Community Chest** – draw a card with immediate effect.
  - **GO** – collect 200 KP when passing.
  - **Jail (Janissary Crisis)** – land or get sent there; must roll doubles or pay to exit.

### 3. Answering Questions
When you buy a property, you must answer a **question** from its category:
- **Did You Know?** – earn points by learning a fact.
- **True or False?** – test your knowledge.
- **What Happened Next?** – choose the correct historical outcome.
- **Two Truths & a Lie** – spot the false statement.

Correct answers give you **Knowledge Points** (the in‑game currency).

### 4. Winning
The game continues until players decide to end (or you can set a target). The player with the **most Knowledge Points** or the **greatest property portfolio** is the victor. A winner banner appears when you choose to reset.

## ✨ Features

- **Fully functional Monopoly board** – correct orientation (no upside‑down cells).
- **40 unique spaces** with historical Tanzimat themes.
- **Four question categories** with dynamic answer buttons.
- **Chance & Community Chest decks** with Ottoman‑flavored events.

- **Language**: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6)

## 📁 File Structure

The game is a **single HTML file** containing all styles, markup, and script. Simply save it as `.html` and open in any browser.

---

## 🛠️ Customization

You can easily modify:
- **Question bank** – edit the `QUESTION_SET` object.
- **Board spaces** – adjust the `BOARD_DATA` array (add/rename properties, change prices).
- **Chance/Community Chest cards** – modify the `CHANCE_CARDS` and `CHEST_CARDS` arrays.
- **Starting money** – change `STARTING_KNOWLEDGE` (currently 1500 KP).
